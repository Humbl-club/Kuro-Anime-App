Kuro · Discover + Clubs · FINAL (binding with the spec) · 3 Nov 2026

Discover is an issue.
Clubs are rooms. Final.

Home is the authority. Discover becomes a daily issue edited from Home’s scene-card language: one cover story on the title’s own artwork material, a contents line, then a few sections with clear hierarchy (a feature, a numbered index, one rail) instead of ten identical poster blocks. Clubs keep the one surface people already love on Home, the dark room, and use it everywhere: the list, the empty page and the club’s own page.

All pages paint Home’s warm canvas (#FFFEF8; manga #F5F5F3) and the header paints the same colour, so swiping from Home no longer jumps to white. Every number, title and line on these frames maps to a field the app already loads. Nothing here needs a backend change.

Binding text: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-discover-clubs-redesign-design.md. Scores: JUDGING.md. Changes from the Editorial mockup: TastePlanKey for Plan, invite and Ask keys for owners and admins only, “Rails” kept in English, one placement per note, “N members” on list rooms, shipped German copied exactly. Frames 5b, 9 and SE-1 are new.

What this page fixes, and where the rules live

  • Binding spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-discover-clubs-redesign-design.md. It has every size, token, string (EN/DE), state, tap target and the lane file split. If this page and the spec disagree, the spec wins.
  • Judging: JUDGING.md. Editorial 8.45, Calm 8.18, Personal-first 7.03.
  • Plan: the existing TastePlanKey / TastePlanController. It plans or unplans with an Undo toast, and a tracked title opens the editor. It is not the +1 KuroAdvanceKey, whose tracked state would be a disabled “Caught up” key.
  • Invite / Ask: owners and admins only (invite_code is withheld from members). A member sees “Add a title” only when a rail is unlocked (frame 5b).
  • Vocabulary: English keeps “rails” and German keeps “Listen”. The tabs are Rails · This week · Polls.
  • Notes: each note appears exactly once. The first one in rail order sits in the hero card, and its tile says “Mina’s note ↑”. Every other note stays under its own tile, behind the spoiler line. No times are shown, because rail notes have none.
  • List rooms: they show “N members”, with initials taken from member_names as Home’s room does. “You” appears only on the club page, where members carry user ids.
  • Not on this page: Collection stays white (owner decision, spec D16), and Browse, Search and Taste are unchanged.
