Kuro · Discover + Clubs · Direction B: personal-first · 2026-11-03

For you first.
Your people, right now.

Discover opens with what is new to this person: their airings today, today's pick as a Home scene card, and New to you as equal leaves. The curated catalogue comes after that and stays editorial. Clubs open on what friends are doing: each club is Home's club room, and a club's first tab is "This week", showing pace and dated updates. Everything is built from Home's components and data the app already loads. There are no backend or flag changes.

Copy

Why personal-first

  • Today the top of Discover is the same for every reader: a lead frame, then ten poster sections at one scale. Home already proves that Kuro feels like Kuro when the page knows you.
  • "Continue" stays on Home. Discover shows only one compact personal glance ("From your list today"), so the two pages never compete. The scene-card weight goes to something new: today's pick.
  • Clubs lose the "four looks" problem (P4). The list, the club page and Home's card are all the same room, and the room leads with the latest human line.

Home is the authority (reused by name)

  • Canvas kuroCanvasHome; manga kuroCanvasHomeManga. The page and KuroMainView.Section.canvas change together, so the header carries no seam (P1).
  • KuroPosterMediumSwitch with KuroArtWordFill, widened to three words; HomeOpeningLineView; the 24/−0.85 section head; the 15/−0.2 in-feed head.
  • HomeComingUpGlance style, HomeSceneCard recipe, HomeMangaLeafMetrics leaves, HomeAlsoWatchingTile, HomeCalendarCard, ClubRoomBackground / ClubRoomPalette / ClubSeal (promoted), KuroAdvanceKey, HomeFooter.

Rules held

  • Only data the app already loads: discover_bundle, DailyFeature, ReleasesStore.upcomingAirings, LibraryStore, SocialActivityStore.friendCount, ClubListRow, ClubBundle.
  • Navigation contract: titles, clubs and See all open journeys (✕ on the root, back when pushed); tools return to their page; no nested targets.
  • Every string in EN/DE (toggle above). Light mode only. Widths come from the measured container: 320 compact, 390, 430, and the 680 iPad column. States use KuroSkeleton / KuroStateView, with one retry style.

Frames are 390 × 844 pt (iPhone 17 Pro). Artwork comes from the prototype's assets (tools/design-directions/assets), with the Kuro storage URLs as fallback. Club members, notes and times are samples.
