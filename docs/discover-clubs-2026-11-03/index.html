Discover + Clubs, in Home's grammar

Home is the design authority. Discover becomes a daily issue: warm canvas, Home's opening row, an All · Anime · Manga switch, one cover story and curated sections. The rails are still hand-picked, and a rail's filter only changes that rail. Clubs keep their structure: each club is a dark room, and a club's page opens on the same room with labelled keys. Before and after are offline fixture renders at 390 pt. Live shows the signed-in dev account on the owned simulator. That account has no clubs, so live Clubs shows the empty page.
