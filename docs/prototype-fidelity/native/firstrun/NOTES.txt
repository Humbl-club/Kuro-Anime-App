Open items for the lead / Task 10 (outside this lane)
1. Busy landscape banner gap. fetch_because_you_rail returns no banner_image, so the busy reference's
   landscape card (banner + premise) appears only when the bundle join hits. A full fix needs the RPC to
   return banner_image (backend change, not prepared here) or a catalog read. Do not accept
   taste-busy-anime-banner-joined-390.png as proof that every busy account looks like the reference.
2. Taste medium switch. TasteOpening uses KuroMediumSwitch(.large) with the first pick's material as a
   gradient. The reference clips the artwork into the selected word and puts the words in a two-column grid
   (MANGA about 6 pt further left, x 202 vs 208). Task 4 owns the switch. Either adopt Task 4's art-filled
   word on Taste after it merges, or record an amendment exempting Taste.
3. Pager canvas. ContentView paints .taste with kuroCanvasHome (#fffef8). TastePicksView paints
   kuroCanvasHomeManga (#f5f5f3) for manga, and its background now ignores the safe area. On iPad the
   .kuroReadableWidth() side margins still show #fffef8 beside #f5f5f3 for manga. Fix in ContentView
   (lift Taste's medium into an owner the pager reads and extend Section.canvas for .taste) or accept.
4. Plan toast host on Home. HomeNewAccount uses the Home page's TastePlanController when HomeView installs
   .tastePlanHost(controller) outside its ScrollView (toast fixed at the screen foot). Without that, the
   block installs its own and the toast draws at the foot of the block. Lane 4 should also latch its
   new-account mount condition (TastePageCopy.latchNewAccount), so the first Plan does not unmount the block.
5. AX5 renders change the process-wide KuroTypeScale for the length of one render and restore it after.
   They run only with KURO_WRITE_SNAPSHOTS=1, so ordinary and full-suite runs never touch the scale.
6. New-account premises. ref-taste-new-manga-390 shows a premise under each new-account pick. The
   Discover bundle rows that feed new-account picks carry no synopsis, so no premise is drawn (never a
   placeholder). Closing this needs a synopsis on the bundle rows (backend) or a per-title read.
7. The new-account latch clears when the Library leaves .loaded or the page is mounted again. The pager
   keeps the current page and its neighbours mounted, so it holds while the user stays near Taste.
