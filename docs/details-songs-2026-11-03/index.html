The lanes were merged with --no-ff in this order: data, platform, detail, songs, widget, profile. The lane worktrees and branches have been removed.

Integration decisions

  • Notifications follow spec D14. The platform lane's first version broke four of the lead's binding choices (D14, §18.3–4). The planner now works like this:
    • A release notifies only when its exact time is known (showsTime) and it is confirmed or announced. It notifies once, at the release moment.
    • A date announced without a time no longer notifies. The lane's 09:00 local rule is removed.
    • Estimates never notify.
    • Each title gets its next release (the account schedule returns one row per title; the planner would take two), with at most 48 notifications pending. A bell turned on for a title later paused, planned, finished or removed goes quiet with it.
    • The notification text follows spec §11.3 in English and German.
  • Reminders are off by default. The Profile toggle turns them on for every title you are watching or reading.
  • The detail bell is a per-title override. It keeps per-account "allowed" and "muted" sets and never changes the Profile toggle.
  • The old English-only airing reminders are retired. These were "… airs now" and "In 1 hour: …".
    • Nothing schedules them any more, and the extra per-write anime read is gone.
    • Pending airing-* requests are removed once per device.
    • Release ids no longer reuse the airing- prefix.
  • Wording now matches this behaviour. The Profile subtitle reads "Confirmed times only, never estimates." / "Nur bestätigte Zeiten, nie Schätzungen.". The privacy policy (§4.7) now says "exact times"; its [prüfen] mark stays. platform/WIDGET.md §4 is updated.
  • Peek fold. The three lane copies were byte-identical. They are now one file, Kuro/Views/Shared/KuroRailPeek.swift, which also holds cardsShown and slice.
  • Tile fold. KuroBrandedTile is not a straight copy, because the lane copies differed:
    • From the entity copy: the associated-value style, the narrow rule (below 60 pt only "KURO") and the initials ("MAPPA" becomes "MA"; one detail test changed).
    • From the detail copy: ink initials sized to the tile.
    • Added: kind words of up to 5 characters ("CH.", "EP. 8") stay on narrow tiles.
  • Songs. DetailSongShelf replaces DetailSoundPanel, which is deleted. songPreview is now injected into the detail test hosts.
  • Plan key. At 98 pt the compact leaf Plan key no longer truncates. It applies only to compact leaf keys, which includes Discover's index rows.
  • UI test. One navigation UI test was updated for the new cast-card label ("name, role") and the new heading "Appears in.".

Review fixes (integration round 2, 3 November evening)

The review list I was given was cut off mid-entry ("Apple worker rate-limit behaviour, file scrip…"). I read that entry as "the Apple pass keeps waiting and retrying for every remaining row when Apple keeps refusing" and fixed it so (the pass stops). Any findings after that entry were not visible to me.

  • Episode timeline (items 2, 3). An episode the release schedule carries is drawn from the schedule (date and certainty mark), never from the catalogue's air date, so the hero and the timeline give the same day, and the rows stay in number order. Only the (at most three) schedule rows that are drawn replace catalogue rows, so every episode still appears exactly once. "Episode 2 - Stray Dog Strut" now reads "Stray Dog Strut". The episode's Watch link has a VoiceOver label ("Watch episode 5", "Opens outside Kuro").
  • Studio "By year." (item 6). Each title in a quiet year is its own full-width row with "›" and wraps inside the column; in a busy year the "N more" count sits outside the truncated titles. The studio render has two long-title fixtures. Under the Rating or Era sort, Works / Roles rows put the year in the meta line instead of the leading year column.
  • Character pages (§9.1). AniList's leading "__Height:__ 190 cm" lines leave the prose and go to the hero facts line (at most two, never age or gender). Staff and author bios are unchanged.
  • Detail hero. On iPad the hero foot (and the manga band) sits in the body's readable column. A title that needs more than two lines beside the mark now stacks the mark under it instead of being cut. The facts line uses Text interpolation (the two iOS 26 deprecation warnings are gone), keeps "·" with the part before it and never splits a part.
  • Rails. More like this and cast cards reserve two title lines, so Plan keys and voice chips line up.
  • Songs (items 7, 8). Preview audio session is .playback / .default as the spec says (a silent phone no longer shows a running 0:30 with no sound), and a stream that is not ready in 10 s fails to "Preview unavailable". Stop instead of pause, and the composition-owned player, are now recorded deviations (spec §17). The About text says that artwork loads from Apple when songs are shown, as the privacy policy does.
  • Widget (item 9). Snapshot text never says "Today" / "Tomorrow" (it would still say "Tomorrow" on the release day while Kuro stays closed); days are absolute. Medium drops the "Up next ·" prefix so the date survives. A queued +1 fades only the face, the glyph stays in ink. A queued +1 caps at the app's own episode or chapter count. A thumbnail fetched after sign-out is never written back to the App Group.
  • Notifications (item 10). A bell override applies only while the title is Watching or Rewatching (otherwise its notifications were unstoppable from the UI). At launch, while the Library is still loading, nothing pending is removed; the plan is made once the Library loads.
  • Import (item 12). A failed history read shows the failure card plus Try again, not "No recent imports.". App-wide: a disabled primary KuroInkButton is now a quiet face with a hairline and quiet ink (about 5 : 1), not white on a 30 % ink face (about 2 : 1).
  • Backend (prepared only). data/BACKFILL.md voice-actor runbook corrected: 2 pages per run, the next startPage is the previous nextStartPage, waves are 50 and 200 runs, and the filter shows skipped/reason (the old "+4 per run" would have skipped 3 of every 4 pages). Two deno tests pin this. New §3a: measure song-artist coverage and re-run the theme pass before the Apple pass. Apple worker: titles must be equal after normalisation (no fuzzy ratio: "COLORS 2" is not "COLORS"), live takes are rejected, the long-vowel folds apply only to romanised Japanese ("Shout" is not "Shot"), and a persistent 403/429 stops the pass.
  • Docs. Spec D17 baseline corrected (about 2,700 pt, not 1,641), §11.2 "next two" marked as built "next one", §15 locale claim corrected (English follows the device locale; the render simulator is en_US with region Germany, hence "Score 8,6"), §17 open items 12–18 added.

Rejected or left as is. Manga read run keeps one row (prototype reference shows one; recorded in §17). Season tab on iPad stays at the art's edge (it belongs to the art). The queued caption wraps to two lines at 8 pt in medium German (legible; the date line was the real loss and is fixed).
