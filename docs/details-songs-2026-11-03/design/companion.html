Companion.

The title page is your watch companion. Where you are and what comes next lead the page. Episodes are a timeline you have walked, with a tick for every one you've watched. The cast is something you can follow from a character to a voice and back. Songs are a short player list. Everything else comes after.

  • Same language as Home: SF sans set by hand, warm papers (#f5f5f4 detail, #f6f5ef manga, #fffef8 Profile/sheets), artwork material only on surfaces that sit on the title's art, 1 pt hairlines, physical keys, Home eyebrows (9 pt, 1.05 tracking), 24 pt −0.85 page headings, and the 17 pt −0.4 detail headings from the prototype.
  • Release wording is ReleaseCopy's, verbatim. A time is shown only when precision = instant. Estimates are always labelled "expected around …", show no time and never notify.
  • No black filter pills and no serif caps on entity, import or Profile pages. Serif stays on the amendment-1 list only (manga title and hook, "Your bookmark", "Seen the anime?").
  • Every sideways rail is sized so a slice of the next card shows: card width = (column − gutter − 3 × gap) ÷ 3.35, which is 98 pt at 390.
  • Sample progress, dates, counts and episode titles are illustrative. Covers and banners are the live Kuro Storage URLs the prototype uses. The song artwork is real Apple artwork, returned by the iTunes Search API on 2026-11-03 for these exact songs. Character portraits are the prototype's own portrait assets, inlined.
