New · Collection page in both app versions

Lists ("Folio")

  • Status words with counts along the top: Watching / Reading, Planned, Completed, Paused, Dropped.
  • Shelf or Index with one tap: wide scenes for anime and equal covers for manga, or compact rows.
  • +1 right on the row with Undo; it stops at the last episode and shows a check when caught up.
  • Next episode or chapter date under each title, sort by Recent, Title, Score or Next release, search across all statuses, batch select (now including Dropped).
Reworked

Profile

  • Your library at a glance: real counts and a cover row per status ("Your lists"); tap a row to open it in Collection.
  • Grouped into Imports & connections, Clubs, Preferences, Account & privacy, and an Advanced section for maintenance tools.
  • New Data sources & credits page (AniList, MangaDex, MangaUpdates, AnimeThemes).
Refined · new Home version (still off for live users)

Home

  • Honest "Coming up": says "Nothing scheduled" only when that's known; otherwise "Dates to come" or "Couldn't load dates" with Try again.
  • Planned titles no longer pose as "in progress"; empty calendar hidden instead of repeating "Nothing scheduled".
  • Neutral colours (the green and cream tints are gone), readable art-filled Anime / Manga words, both words stay whole at large text sizes.
Better · plus new sections

Title pages

  • Next release block (episode, chapter or volume) labelled confirmed, announced or expected, and a songs section with Listen in Apple Music or Spotify. Both appear once the prepared server changes are live.
  • The floating action bar no longer covers the synopsis; year and format shown once; one button style.
  • German throughout, no internal words ("verified", "catalog fallback"), neutral provider chips instead of brand colours.
Refined

Discover & Search

  • Discover in plain "issue" frames on white: picture, serif title, one or two lines. No glass panel, no score chips.
  • "Airing today" sorted by air time; manga rails get See all; loads once per launch instead of twice.
  • Search opens on the medium you're in, shows your recent searches and a way into Browse; refinements are ink words.
Refined

Clubs

  • A failed or offline load now says so, instead of showing "Create a club" as if you had none.
  • Club cards use real artwork from the club's titles; unread is a small ink dot; the club room is neutral dark grey.
  • Invite links keep the code exactly as written, and the share text includes the link again.
Rebranded · safer

Sign in & sign up

  • The approved welcome screen is unchanged. The form now uses ink instead of red and blue accents, scales with text size, and Return moves between fields.
  • New: choose a new password after a reset link. Before, a reset link simply signed you in.
  • Sign-in links are accepted only when this phone asked for them. Clear errors in English and German.
Rebuilt

Onboarding

  • Three short frames in the welcome screen's style instead of five icon pages that described screens that no longer exist.
  • Choose Anime or Manga (Collection opens on it), bring your list from AniList or skip, and learn that clubs are private.
  • Remembered per account, not per phone.
Light polish

Taste & Browse

  • Ink type and tokens, proper loading, empty and failed states, two grid columns at large text sizes.
  • Still to polish: some Taste blurbs come from the data in lowercase, and Browse's filter row runs to the edge.
New building blocks

States & screen sizes

  • One set of honest messages for loading, empty, offline and failed, used across screens.
  • Lists checked on the smallest iPhone width (320 pt) and the largest (430 pt).
