- Device: `7FC4B58A-…` "iPhone 17 Pro Kuro Qualification", light appearance, status bar overridden to 9:41 (override cleared at the end).

## Capture caveats (read first)

1. **In World B, `--kuro-start=` is ignored.** `ContentView` `.onChange(of: flags.seriesHomeNativeV1Enabled)` sets `selection = .home` once the foundation flags hydrate, which is under about 1.4 s after launch. That overrides the start argument. **Verified:** a cold-start `kuro://collection` opened about 0.3 s after launch also ends on Home. In the flag-on world, launch-time deep links (collection, discover, and possibly detail sheets) are swallowed by this redirect. This happens on every launch, not only when the flag first rolls out. As a result, the World B taste, discover, collection and clubs captures were all identical to Home (same md5). I deleted the B-taste and B-clubs captures, which were mislabelled copies of Home. I re-took B-discover and B-collection with `kuro://discover` and `kuro://collection` after the app had settled. No deep link exists for Taste or the Clubs list, so World B has **no Taste or Clubs capture**. Those views are the same views as in World A; only their position in the pager changes.
2. **Deep-link ids are Kuro's internal `id`s, not AniList ids.** `CatalogStore` looks titles up with `.eq("id", …)`. So `kuro://anime/21` opened *Assassination Classroom* (not One Piece), `anime/1535` opened *The Garden of Sinners Ch. 6* (not Death Note), and `manga/30013` opened *Almight Network* (not One Piece). The file names keep the numeric ids. Any shared or marketing link built from AniList ids will open the wrong title.
3. **No Profile or Search capture.** No launch argument or deep link opens either, and tapping is not possible with `simctl`.
4. **The first B-home-AX capture showed Manga mode**, because the Home medium setting from B-home-manga persisted. I kept it as `B-home-manga-AX.png` and re-took `B-home-AX.png` in Anime mode.

## Top 10 issues

1. **The Home medium title's picture fill has no contrast floor** (B-home, B-home-manga, AX). It is a deliberate mask effect (`HomeView.swift:288`), but pale artwork erases strokes: the "M" of Manga disappears and "Anime" looks mottled. Fix: darken or desaturate the fill, add a luminance clamp, or add a thin `kuroInk` stroke or shadow under the masked lettering.
2. **Accessibility header** (all AX shots). The wordmark disappears, the section pill is off-centre and the search glyph overflows its circle. Fix: cap the header at `.dynamicTypeSize(...DynamicTypeSize.xxxLarge)` or use a fixed-size icon frame, and keep the pill centred with a fixed layout.
3. **Detail action dock covers content** (all detail shots). The synopsis is cut in half behind it and the tag pills ghost through. Fix: add bottom content inset equal to the dock height plus margin, or give the dock an opaque background.
4. **Duplicate metadata line on detail** ("2015 • TV" plus "SCORE … · 2015 · TV …"). Fix: drop the first line or remove year and format from the second, and close the gap.
5. **Berserk continue card in Manga mode**: a half-width single card, the overlay overlapping the poster, a double border and a filled "+". Fix: use the same full-width continue card as Anime mode.
6. **Home tab layout differs from Taste/Browse/Collection** (Manga right-aligned and larger; truncated to "Ma…" at AX). Fix: one shared medium-tab component.
7. **Taste blurbs use non-synopsis copy** ("let's break it down.", lowercase "vash…"). Fix: fall back to the synopsis's first sentence and capitalise it; align "+ Plan" to the card bottom.
8. **Clipped horizontal controls** (Collection "Paused (", Browse "All genres"). Fix: add a trailing fade or inset so the last item is visibly scrollable.
9. **Detail dock buttons use mixed shapes**, and the manga read button looks disabled. Fix: one shape family; if the button is unavailable, hide it or label why.
10. **Repeated empty states on Home** ("Nothing scheduled" twice) and the blank lower half of Collection. Fix: hide Calendar when it is empty, or merge the two messages; add a quiet footer to short lists.

Functional notes for owners (not visual): in the flag-on world, hydration forces Home at launch and swallows the start argument and cold-start deep links (caveat 1, `ContentView` `.onChange(of: flags.seriesHomeNativeV1Enabled)`). Deep links also use internal ids rather than AniList ids (caveat 2).
