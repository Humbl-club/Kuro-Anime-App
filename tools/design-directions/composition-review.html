Rebuild the composition first.

  • Use a neutral canvas while testing hierarchy. Artwork supplies the colour.
  • Keep the familiar top pager; integrate medium switching into a compact control.
  • Make updated titles stand out through placement and readable labels.
  • Differentiate Anime and Manga through image proportions and progress units; share typography and controls.
  • Put club context beside the relevant title, with a fuller section below.

Acceptance targets for the next study

Within the opening viewAt least four identifiable active titles, direct progress controls and the start of a personal update section at 390 × 844.
Within the next viewSeveral upcoming releases and a relevant club activity item. No single-title bottleneck.
Across both mediaOne compact switch, readable text and recognisable artwork. No page-count or fake-book visual requirement.
Before visual sign-offCompare the complete phone screen with one agreed reference for tone. Test busy, new and quiet accounts.
