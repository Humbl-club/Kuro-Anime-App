A swipe selects minutes; a tap starts. Fast scrolling lands at useful values. When the countdown passes zero it becomes elapsed time.

For Home: preserve direct scroll control and clear stopping points. A list should never be slowed down to show an animation. A timer dial is not a template for a content feed.

What we still need to make visible

Proposed Kuro Home work, not claims about !Boring timings or finished native behavior.

Surface

A clear depth order

Background → shared tray → artwork → action. One light source; fewer arbitrary floating containers.

Touch

The whole action

Immediate contact, release-to-commit, graceful cancellation, a brief settled result. No delayed tap just for theatre.

Hierarchy

Recognize before reading

Cover first, title second, progress next. Club context stays attached to its title, without hiding the cover.

Scroll

Motion inside artwork

Keep controls anchored and readable. Anime artwork has a brief fluid entrance; Manga artwork has a clean reveal. Sections and buttons stay fixed, and Reduced Motion removes both.

Medium switch

One shared mechanism

The same selection should drive the label, rail and material transition together. Preserve quick tap and direct drag.

Feedback

Quiet in public

Restrained success feedback, explicit failed state and Undo. Native haptics later; browser previews cannot establish how they feel.

How we review the next Home pass

Visible success criteria
  • Recognize active titles and next progress action without reading a paragraph.
  • No accidental progress change from vertical scrolling or cancelled presses.
  • Resting, pressed, completed and disabled controls are distinguishable.
  • Multiple simultaneous releases remain visible; artwork keeps its full shape.
  • Animation reinforces a change and never blocks scrolling or makes text disappear.
  • Reduce Motion keeps every control understandable and usable.
