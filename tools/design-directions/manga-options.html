Edition

Full preview ↗

Ivory spreads. Prominent covers. Chapter progress in the margin.

Design & interaction decisions

Two priority reads have the most visual space. Each is one publication spread: cover, title, a large chapter number, a quiet circular key and a named reader line. Four other reads use a compact continuation index.

Release dates become editorial headings. Sunday Club retains its own dark identity. Progress uses the existing update, cancellation, completion and Undo behavior, keeping nearby covers steady.

Best for a personal reading space. Tradeoff: the two priority titles receive noticeably more attention than the rest.

Full preview ↗

Neutral ink. Six cover plates. Small, precise progress labels.

Design & interaction decisions

All six current reads share the same cover geometry, clear title and pale chapter label. Artwork supplies the color; a neutral ink surface holds it together. Progress controls stay separate from opening a title.

Upcoming titles form date-grouped image contact sheets, supporting several simultaneous releases. The lighter club note sits inside its private dark room, rather than blending into the gallery.

Best for artwork recognition. Tradeoff: every cover gets generous space, so reaching releases takes more vertical scrolling. Native dark presentation is not currently implemented in Kuro.

A compact reading journal. Covers, names and chapter cursors.

Design & interaction decisions

Every ongoing title gets one aligned line: intact cover, editorial title, reader context and a chapter cursor at the right. The chapter number and advance key share one consistent vertical margin.

Hairlines do the organising, not boxed cards. Releases keep the same chronological axis; Sunday Club becomes a contrasting private space. No new Home submenus or hidden current reads.

Best for scanning a larger list. Tradeoff: covers are smaller and the presentation is more functional than the other two.
