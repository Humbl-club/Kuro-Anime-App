Official source ↗

Group all followed releases by day; keep new discoveries distinct from personal follow-ups.

What I would leave behind

The large selected-title panel and horizontal rails for every section.

What this changes for Kuro

Anime feels cinematic.

Several current shows. Proper wide artwork. A small progress action on each. No single-show takeover.

Manga feels collected.

Full covers, visible chapter progress and a tighter composition. Same typography and controls; a different rhythm.

Home stays personal.

One continuous scroll: active titles, all followed releases, relevant new releases, then club context. No context submenus.
