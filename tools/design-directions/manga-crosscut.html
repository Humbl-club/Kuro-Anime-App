What changed — and why
  • Two interlocking panels make each row. The narrower one starts lower; their chapter edges meet on the same baseline.
  • Chapter controls occupy a cut in the artwork. Opening a story and marking a chapter are separate, reachable actions.
  • Names and shared-club readers sit in the white gutter. No additional descriptive copy on current reads.
  • Widths alternate at each row; reading order stays left to right. This rhythm distinguishes Manga from Anime’s landscape scenes.
  • Existing release chronology and Sunday Club are retained. One press updates the chapter in place; Undo and failed updates use the existing prototype behavior.
