MY RECOMMENDATION FOR THE NEXT STUDY

Savee’s composition.
Family’s attention to touch.

Bring in !Boring for one or two memorable actions. Use Gentler’s varied composition to keep releases and club activity readable.

  • Colour lives with the content. Try a restrained cover-derived tint, with stable readable text and monochrome status controls.
  • Containers do different jobs. An ongoing title, release group and club comment should not all be the same rounded rectangle.
  • Motion connects states. Preserve the cover’s position as details open; preserve scroll position when closing.
  • Polish has a specification. Image crops, baseline alignment, layered edges, pressed states, disabled states, reduced motion and failure feedback.
