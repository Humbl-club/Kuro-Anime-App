Library · reworked

Review proposal ↗

Equal covers. Each read has its own bookmark.

Cover/title → details · + → next chapter

Design philosophy & tradeoff

The chosen-book workbench is removed following owner rejection. Every read has its own current chapter and 44px advance key. Cover and title open details; chapter changes never require selecting a title or opening its page first.

Tradeoff: six chapter keys are visible, but there is no remote control bar or extra Open row. Equal artwork sizing, release calendar and Sunday Club remain. Rework is a proposal, not owner-approved native implementation.

Chapter board

Open ↗

Progress first. Six reads inside one precise instrument.

Advance any read directly · artwork opens details

Design philosophy & tradeoff

One shared panel holds six equal cells. Cover thumbnails identify titles; an unread count answers how much remains in the currently available chapters. Current position, total and advance are aligned below. Unknown totals say “Total pending”; they never become zero.

This treats Home as a useful daily dashboard rather than a bookstore. Real progress bars appear only for known totals. Each read has one direct action, with club readers alongside.

Tradeoff: less artwork space, more tracking information. Best for people reading many series concurrently and checking what they can catch up on.

Reading journal

Open ↗

Continuity first. A personal reading rhythm, not a gallery.

One vertical flow · recency labels are sample data

Design philosophy & tradeoff

Current reads are organised by recency: last opened, earlier this week, and still in rotation. Each gets the same cover window, editorial title and immediate chapter key. A continuous hairline connects the activity groups.

Titles lead rather than chapter statistics. Club readers and new chapters sit with the relevant read, so reopening a story follows your own history. There are no sorting tabs or hidden carousels.

Tradeoff: it is taller, and actual recency requires reliable user activity data. The preview recency groups are invented fixtures, not imported account history.

Club room

Open ↗

Belonging first. Shared reading has its own private table.

Named readers → private club · solo reads stay visible

Design philosophy & tradeoff

A bounded Sunday Club table groups your current reads that overlap with the club. Every cover window has the same size. Named readers, chapters and update keys stay with each title. Personal reads continue underneath with identical cover geometry.

This changes the organising principle from title priority to shared context. The connected table, member marks and lock distinguish it from the rest of Home. The existing Sunday Club thought appears later as the conversation area.

Tradeoff: sorting by social overlap can displace the most recently read title. A real implementation needs per-club permission-aware activity, privacy settings and explicit treatment of multiple clubs.

Library was selected by Max. The other three remain unselected proposals. These are local browser designs, not native app changes. Poster, Anime, release chronology and Sunday Club discussions use the existing prototype. Physical touch, backend data and durable offline writes are not represented.
